# Comparisons stay inside one price table

Monitor Pangan publishes each commodity as its own price table: one trading date, a price per province, and each province's gap versus the average. The page also offers other commodities, other dates, and a source line that the figure is not today's price. A price question is answered from exactly one table. The reply names the trading date and does not call the price today. Provinces with no price are left out of a ranking. Cross-commodity comparisons and trends across dates are not price questions.

A single-province-only agent was rejected because the table already ranks. Trends and cross-commodity answers were rejected because they are not one published table, and a model will narrate past the numbers.
